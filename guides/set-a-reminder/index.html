4 simple steps

  1. Write what you don’t want to forget

    Open the SlideInk panel and tap the keyboard icon. Type, for example, “Call Peter about the car”.

  2. Pick a time

    Tap the bell and choose when the reminder should go off. For example in an hour, in the evening or tomorrow. You can also set your own date and time.

  3. Get it done or snooze it

    When the reminder appears, you can tap Done. If it doesn’t suit you right now, use Snooze to pick a later time.

  4. See your saved reminders

    Open the Library and choose Reminders. You’ll find both scheduled and completed reminders there.

Tips

  • When SlideInk asks for permission to show notifications, allow it.
  • Also turn on precise reminders if the app offers them. Without this permission a reminder may arrive late.
  • If the reminder should repeat regularly, use the Repeat option.
  • On the lock screen you may see only “Reminder”. Whether you also see its text depends on your phone’s settings.

Can you do it without SlideInk?

Yes. You can use a calendar or another reminder app. With SlideInk you write the reminder right over what you have open.

Frequently asked questions

Are reminders free?

From your first saved reminder you can create them free for 14 days. If you want to keep adding new ones after that, you can buy SlideInk PRO. You pay only once, with no subscription. Reminders you’ve already saved keep working.

Can I write a reminder by hand?

In the panel, type it on the keyboard. SlideInk doesn’t turn a handwritten note into a reminder.

What if the reminder doesn’t suit me right now?

Tap Snooze and choose when it should remind you again.

Which devices does SlideInk work on?

On phones and tablets with Android 7 or newer. When you first start it, allow it to display over other apps so the panel can open over them.
