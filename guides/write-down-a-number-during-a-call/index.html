4 simple steps

  1. Open the panel

    Tap the SlideInk handle at the edge of the screen or drag it towards the middle.

  2. Write the number down

    Write it with your finger or a stylus right in the note. You can keep talking while you do.

  3. Save the note

    Tap the floppy disk icon – Save Note. You’ll then find the note in the Library.

  4. Collapse the panel

    Carry on with the call. You can come back to the saved note any time.

Tips

  • Turn on the speaker or use headphones so you can write during the call.
  • If you prefer typing, tap the keyboard icon.
  • You can note an appointment, an address, a name or an order number the same way.

Can you do it without SlideInk?

Yes. During a call you can open another notes app and write the number down there. The call will keep going. With SlideInk, the note opens right over the call screen.

Frequently asked questions

Is SlideInk free?

Yes. Notes and drawing are free. You can try reminders free for 14 days. If you want to keep creating new ones after that, you can buy SlideInk PRO. You pay only once, with no subscription.

Will the other person see what I write?

No. In a regular phone call, only you can see the note.

Which devices does SlideInk work on?

On phones and tablets with Android 7 or newer. When you first start it, allow it to display over other apps. That’s what lets you open a note during a call.
